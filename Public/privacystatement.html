4. The nature of your responses

The Tool asks about how you relate to your own judgment, successes, and setbacks. It is not a clinical, diagnostic, or health assessment, and your responses are not used, and are not intended, to diagnose, treat, or make any health-related, employment-related, or other significant decision about you. We treat your responses as standard personal data on this basis. If you have a concern about the sensitivity of a specific response, you are welcome to skip the optional reflection fields, and you may withdraw entirely at any time (Section 10).

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the Tool. Each acts as a data processor on our behalf, under their own terms:

  • Google (Google Sheets / Apps Script) — stores your responses and, if provided, sends the saved-results email. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify — hosts the website you're using right now. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • Calendly — only if you choose to book a discovery call; at that point you leave this Tool's data environment and Calendly's own privacy policy applies. See Calendly's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any other third party for their own marketing purposes.

8. Automated processing

Your scores are calculated automatically from your answers. This calculation does not produce any legal effect or similarly significant decision about you, no automated decision is made that affects your rights, opportunities, or access to anything, and no human or automated judgement is made about you as a result. The results are provided for your own reflection only.

9. Age

The Tool is intended for adults aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please do not submit responses without the involvement of a parent or guardian.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase your data ("the right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict how we process it.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests (where relevant).
  • Port your data to another provider in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority, if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 1 or Section 11. We will respond within the timeframes required by law.
